A fictional studio favorite that takes five minutes.

## Ingredients

### Base

- 1 cup spinach
- 1 banana
- 1 cup oat milk

### Finish

- 1 tablespoon chia seeds

## Instructions

1. Blend the base until smooth.
2. Stir in the chia seeds and rest for two minutes.
